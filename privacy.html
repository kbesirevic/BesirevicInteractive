Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

This policy explains what information besirevicinteractive.com (the "site") collects and why. The site is run by Beširević Interactive LLC ("we", "us"). If you have any questions, email hello@besirevicinteractive.com.

What we collect

The site has no accounts, forms or comments, and we don't ask for your name or email. The only information collected is anonymous usage data through Google Analytics, and only if you accept analytics cookies.

If you accept, Google Analytics records things like:

  • the pages you view and how far you scroll;
  • clicks on links that leave the site (for example to our X profile);
  • your approximate location (country or city, derived from your IP address);
  • your device type, browser, operating system and screen size;
  • how you arrived at the site (for example a search engine or a link on social media).

We use this only to understand how many people visit the site and what they look at. We don't use it for advertising, and we don't sell it. Google states that Google Analytics 4 does not log or store IP addresses.

If you accept, Google Analytics sets these cookies:

The site also remembers your choice in your browser's local storage (bi-consent), so the banner doesn't appear on every visit. This stays on your device.

Your choices

  • Change your choice at any time: (also at the bottom of the home page). Declining removes the analytics cookies.
  • Block or delete cookies in your browser settings.
  • Use Google's Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Who processes the data

Analytics data is processed by Google LLC on our behalf and may be processed in the United States. See how Google uses information from sites that use its services. Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months and then deleted automatically.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may record visitors' IP addresses in its server logs for security purposes; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Children

The site is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access or delete data about you, or to object to its use. Because the analytics data is not linked to your name or contact details, we usually can't identify which data belongs to you, but you can contact us at hello@besirevicinteractive.com and we'll help where we can.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
